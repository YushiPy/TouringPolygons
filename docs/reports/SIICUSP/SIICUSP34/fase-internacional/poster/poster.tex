  \block{background-head}{42.7}{17.7}{38.4}{\head{2}{Background and contribution}}
  \block{background}{42.7}{19.50}{38.4}{\tagtext A polynomial subproblem\par\vspace{0.15cm}\bodytext Both variants are NP-hard for non-convex polygons, but the fixed-order TPP with convex polygons is polynomial [1, 2] with fixed source $s$ and target $t$; a TSPN tour has no fixed point.\par\vspace{1.0cm}\tagtext The state of the art\par\vspace{0.15cm}\bodytext Fekete et al. [3] solve the TSPN by branch and bound. Each convex subproblem is a second-order cone program (SOCP) solved by Gurobi, a commercial optimizer, within a tolerance.\par\vspace{1.0cm}\tagtext Our contribution\par\vspace{0.15cm}\bodytext We build on the last-step shortest-path maps of Dror et al. [1], later improved by Tan and Jiang [2], to obtain an exact algorithm for the \textbf{fixed-order closed tour} through convex polygons, which may touch or overlap, with \textbf{zero error on rational input}. It is the geometric oracle of our \textbf{exact branch-and-bound solver for the TSPN}, in place of the SOCP solver of [3]. For speed, floating-point bounds are proved with directed rounding; the exact stage runs only when needed. Ours is faster on every instance, with a \textbf{71× median speedup} over [3].}
