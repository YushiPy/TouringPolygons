# Extensões: referências

Problemas vizinhos do TSPN que podem usar o oráculo. Os PDFs ficam só na cópia
local (`docs/extensions/**/*.pdf` é ignorado): são artigos de terceiros, e
alguns proíbem redistribuição.

- **AngleTSP.** S. Asaeedi e M. Soheil Shamaee, *Angle constrained Paths*,
  preprint na Research Square (CC BY 4.0). Local: `AngleTSP/Angle_constrained_Paths.pdf`.
- **CETSP.** W. P. Coutinho, R. Q. do Nascimento, A. A. Pessoa e
  A. Subramanian, *A Branch-and-Bound Algorithm for the Close-Enough Traveling
  Salesman Problem*, INFORMS Journal on Computing 28(4):752–765, 2016,
  doi:10.1287/ijoc.2016.0711. Local: `CETSP/coutinho2016.pdf`.
- **CVRPN.** E. G. Birgin e R. S. Matos, *Solving the capacitated vehicle
  routing problem with polygonal neighborhoods using a metaheuristic enhanced
  with block coordinate descent*, manuscrito, 2026. Local: `CVRPN/bma2025-cvrpn.pdf`.
- **CVTSP.**
  - B. S. Vieira, E. M. Frazzon e L. C. Coelho, *An Exact Linearization-Based
    Refinement Algorithm to the Carrier-Vehicle Traveling Salesman Problem*,
    Transportation Science 60(4):589–607, 2026, doi:10.1287/trsc.2025.0292
    ([`bib.md`](CVTSP/bib.md)); versão CIRRELT-2025-22 em `CVTSP/cirrelt-2025-22.pdf`.
  - G. Erdoğan e E. A. Yıldırım, *Exact and Heuristic Algorithms for the
    Carrier-Vehicle Traveling Salesman Problem*, Transportation Science
    55(1):101–121, 2021, doi:10.1287/trsc.2020.0999. Versão aceita (CC BY-NC)
    em `CVTSP/cvtsp_TS_Savelsbergh_suggestions_25_apr_2020.pdf`.
- **FSTSP.** C. C. Murray e A. G. Chu, *The flying sidekick traveling salesman
  problem: Optimization of drone-assisted parcel delivery*, Transportation
  Research Part C 54:86–109, 2015, doi:10.1016/j.trc.2015.03.005. Local:
  `FSTSP/1-s2.0-S0968090X15000844-main.pdf`.
